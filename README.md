# E o Balaio?

E o Balaio helps you follow the current positions of buses for a chosen
line on an interactive map. Vehicle information is supplied by the Mobilibus
real-time feed and is refreshed automatically.

## What you can do

- View buses currently reporting position on the map.
- Choose a line from the list of available lines.
- Type a line number or name to find it quickly.
- Refresh vehicle data whenever you need it.
- See your own position and buses near you, if you allow location access.

## Following a bus line

The line panel is displayed at the bottom of the map.

1. Browse the line chips horizontally, then tap a line to follow it.
2. The map centers on the active buses for that line.
3. Each green bus marker represents a vehicle reporting its position.
4. Tap and drag the map or use pinch-to-zoom to explore the area.

The panel reports the number of active buses and, when available, the trip
destination of the selected line.

## Searching for a line

Use the search field labeled **“Digite a linha”** in the line panel.

- Enter all or part of a line identifier, such as `8551` or `S66`.
- Matching line chips appear as you type.
- Tap a result to select it.
- If you enter the exact line identifier, press the keyboard search/Enter key
  to select it immediately.
- Use the **×** icon to clear the search.

## Hiding and restoring the line panel

Tap the down-arrow in the top-right corner of the line panel to slide it out
of view and give the map more space.

While the panel is hidden, a floating magnifying-glass button appears in the
bottom-right corner. Tap it to bring the line search panel back.

## My location and nearby buses

Tap the floating location button on the right side of the map to use **Minha
localização**.

- The app asks for location permission only after you tap this button.
- When permission is granted, your position is shown by a blue marker and the
  map centers on it.
- The line panel shows how many reporting buses are within 1.5 km and the
  distance to the nearest bus.

You can continue using the app without granting location permission. The map
will still show buses and selected lines; only the personal-location and
nearby-bus features are unavailable.

## Refreshing live data

The app automatically checks for new vehicle positions every 20 seconds. The
header shows when the data was last updated.

To refresh immediately, tap the circular refresh button in the top-right
corner. If the feed cannot be reached, the app displays a retry action and
keeps any previously loaded map data visible when possible.

## Important notes

- A marker shows the latest position reported by a bus, not a guaranteed
  second-by-second GPS track.
- The number of available lines and vehicles depends on what the real-time
  provider is publishing at that moment.
- Location permission is used only to center the map and calculate nearby
  buses; it is not required to follow a line.
